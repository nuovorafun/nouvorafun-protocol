    Principles

    Discretion above all.

    Yours to hold

    Material you generate remains with its owner, retained solely for the owner's return.

    Yours to steer

    Controls are within easy reach; defaults lean toward revealing less about you.

    Yours to erase

    Discarding a thread discards whatever grew from it. No ghost copies linger.

    Yours to share

    Sharing is a decision, not a default. A door opens solely on your word.
