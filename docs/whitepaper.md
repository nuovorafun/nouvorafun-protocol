# Nuovora Whitepaper

## Vision

A workspace where the person using it is the owner of what comes out of it. Conversation, creation and building should feel like thinking aloud in a quiet room, with nobody taking notes on you.

## Mission

Give people a confidential place to talk, make and build, and give them real control over what is kept, what is erased and what is shared. Run it on Robinhood Chain so participation and ownership can grow in the open.

## Principles

- Yours to hold: material belongs to the person who made it.
- Yours to steer: defaults lean toward saying less about you.
- Yours to erase: removing something removes what grew out of it.
- Yours to share: publishing is always a choice.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation

Stand up the brand, the site and the live market view. Settle the contract details and record them.

### Phase 2: Open doors

Invite a first circle of users, watch how they work and tune the three lanes around those habits.

### Phase 3: Widen the circle

Add ownership features and on-chain ways to take part, with the same confidentiality defaults.

### Phase 4: Long horizon

Hand more of the steering to the people who use the workspace every day.
